00:04:08  How to interpret these measured gains

These are qualified annotation-recovery results, not whole-graph precision. Shared sources and prior reference use limit independence. Physical association is candidate context, not confirmed direct binding. Additional primary learning benefit over fully refreshed annotations remains uncertain.
