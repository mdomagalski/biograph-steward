Interpretation and source qualification

Whole sequences, description conditioning, vocabulary, training dates and model components differ together. This compares practical published profiles and does not isolate one causal mechanism. NK/LK does not establish training-unseen sequences, homology separation or Pharos Tdark. E5 dates and training-overlap limitations make this retrospective development. Reference-unmatched forecasts are unresolved, not biological negatives; Fmax maximizes an evaluation threshold rather than measuring percent scientific accuracy.
