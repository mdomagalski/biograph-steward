Reference and mapping qualifications

HOU supplies 3,814 released test proteins; 31 match the 99 original primary targets by exact accession. The other 68 targets lack this reference. Unreported compartments are not negative labels. Fifty-three lossy author-map assignments were excluded before the join using fixed ontology-scope checks, including “membrane → plasma membrane.” The unchanged coarse-map control remains above and is not a biologically qualified localization arm.

The inspected author notebook can admit an OpenCell-only label through its consensus function. The released CSV lacks per-label source masks, so dual-source support is unconfirmed; we do not assert that this affected the 31 matched proteins. The benchmark labels themselves were left unchanged.

Experimental-only filtering loses two supported labels in two proteins relative to the all-code native arm. Evidence codes describe evidence type; this result does not make TAS annotations false.

Human-reviewed cancer claim–evidence matches

Two distinct current CIViC Evidence Items exactly match the published five-field claim, full therapy set, disease, PMID and evidence paragraph. Their final human-reviewed labels are both SUPPORTS. The raw typed human corrections and released final labels agree.

All 46 evidence records / 41 assertions stay in scope. This adds two gold-labelled claim projections beyond the captured Pharos-only graph, which contains none of these reported-response claims. One human reviewer and the same CIViC/study lineage are disclosed. Additional clinical qualifiers and the complete assertion remain unassessed; candidate review states have not been promoted.

The full sequence-qualified paired subset contains only four negative pairs. Its AUROC improvement reverses in one negative-study omission. The broader assay-context panel remains below chance in both networks; it is an adverse proxy check, not a validated physical-binding classifier.

On mapped known labels in that broader full-frame panel, physical-network presence reduces negative-record conflicts from 492 to 330, while positive hits fall from 4,889 to 4,300. This trades recall for fewer conflicting calls. The 5,197 identity-unavailable cases and 20 conflicting reference rows remain visible in the saved result.

STRING physical association includes co-complex evidence. Neither score is a universal direct-binding probability. High average precision in the nearly all-positive canonical subset mostly reflects prevalence; all AP values, missingness, the historical STRING 11.0 comparator and study-omission checks are in the numerical appendix.

Additional checks, including results that did not improve

MitoCarta maps to 2 resident-inventory positives and 1 explicit “mito-interacting” outside-inventory category among 98 primary gene units. All four fixed arms give the same result: two positive hits and one outside-inventory conflict. The other 95 units remain unknown. No improvement is claimed.

DrugCentral agrees with 168 of 174 target–drug source records by exact human accession and native drug name; this is source agreement, not independent gold. ClinGen supplies expert disease-validity labels but matches none of the 100 current disease-association records by exact gene and disease. Broader disease names are not substituted.

The candidate register also records rejected transfers: synthetic DisGeNET labels, CKG-specific BioKGBench judgments, different EvidenceNet extraction assertions, and a published expert-labelled dataset for which no public label artifact was located. This is a documented search, not a claim that every possible public dataset has been exhausted.

What changed in the project

Offline, source-pinned reference adapters and reusable preparation tools now check exact evidence, canonical sequences, conflicting labels and missing scores. Fixed profiles, all comparisons and unfavorable outcomes are retained. No language model assigns the reference labels; no graph assertion is accepted or pruned automatically.

These results complement the separate measured function-prediction improvements. They do not turn Fmax into graph accuracy or prove that every enriched graph relationship is correct. The original 200-case whole-workspace audit remains unchanged.
