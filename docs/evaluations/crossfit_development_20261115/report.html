Method and limits

This is one combined input-profile change: the ordered parents are historical LocalPlus and fitting-label annotation frequency; DeepGO-SE is absent from this new arm. The unchanged eight-layer native GCN receives the original 40 Boolean source-context channels. It is not a full ProtBoost reproduction or an isolated causal estimate of an OOF correction.

The same 4,040 fitting proteins, 208,835 positive annotation memberships, 3,557 root-positive training rows and 27,942 model output terms are retained. Fitting, validation and final context snapshots remain May18,2023; September4,2025; and April10,2026, respectively. No source refresh or new target annotations enter this arm.

Five exact full-sequence GroupKFold partitions exclude each fitting fold from its graph-parent seed table and prior/frequency calculations. Complement priors include every complementary fitting row before root filtering. Validation and final features use all original 4,040 fitting rows. The original literal source head vocabulary remains available even when a fold has zero positive seeds; alias scores are combined by native MAX after scoring.

Observed annotation frequency is not verified biological prevalence or a PU class prior. Missing labels remain zero in the unchanged benchmark BCE. No positive-unlabeled loss or custom correction is included. Exact-sequence exclusion does not establish distant-family, study or GOA-source independence.

Native model layers, loss, optimizer, twenty epochs and best-three SWA remain unchanged. Normal checkpoint selection uses the already-consumed September2025–March2026 validation. No final-reference label is a fitting or forecast input, and no final-reference result selects this fixed arm.

All 22 earlier methods and all 192 earlier contrasts are retained alongside the 132 new contrasts. Saved scalar readouts and intervals are copied; observed point subtraction is display arithmetic only. No interval, threshold, winner or new success gate is selected by this renderer.

Later curator annotations measure annotation recovery, not independent experiments, publication novelty or the percentage of biologically correct whole-graph Pharos assertions. Unmatched predictions are not established biological falsehoods. GOA-source reuse and incomplete annotations remain limitations.

The reference was already consumed before this development comparison. Earlier failed and favorable approaches remain visible, and any gain needs independent confirmation.
