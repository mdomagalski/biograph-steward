# Project alignment

This crosswalk connects the delivered application to the user-supplied organizer brief, proposal, final-presentation email and Slack excerpts. It records implemented functions and their scope, rather than organizer approval or an estimated score. Full dated assessments remain in the [experiment ledger](EXPERIMENT_HISTORY.md).

BioGraph Steward helps scientists follow a **question → plan → source query → focused evidence graph → source inspection → review proposals** workflow without graph-query syntax. The [source agent](SOURCE_AGENT.md) uses native STRING physical associations, native GO locations and qualified archived pathways. Deterministic adapters validate scope and execute each query. The model prioritizes citations and selects source-bound proposals for human decision. It does not silently accept or prune biological claims.

- **Public application:** [open the recorded KRAS investigation](https://bi-3a117d5e2b3942678dc518fce65ab5d2.ecs.us-east-2.on.aws/?run=native_evidence_workspace_20261115&question=native_physical_KRAS_question_v3_20261115&agent_job=source_agent_eval_dc7b597201da_S01&view=data) without sign-in, a token, an AWS account or a running model. Fresh construction, retrieval, inference and review edits are disabled on this read-only demonstration. New supported work runs in a local or authorized operator installation.
- **Added source context:** the native 99-target workspace includes 330 sequence-qualified physical pair records: 243 overlap captured baseline pairs and 87 are absent from that frame. The existing agent's E002 passport shows KRAS–CDC42BPB, its native score, exact source and baseline-absence flag. These are captured-frame source additions, not discoveries or independent experiment counts. See the [audited additions](evaluations/source_agent_20261116/demonstrated_additions.json).
- **Applied qualification:** the separate retained example has 16,687 records after excluding 298 records with obsolete GO identifiers from 16,985 inputs. Its original baseline, every decision and unresolved retained records remain recoverable. This is an applied identifier-currency rule, not a claim of 298 false biological relationships.
- **Agent fidelity:** [five supported queries](evaluations/SOURCE_AGENT_RESULTS_20261116.md), including a valid empty accepted-only result, exactly preserve deterministic query/answer/focused-graph outputs. Three other requests stop at preflight without model calls. This establishes bounded workflow fidelity, separate from unrestricted routing or prediction improvement.
- **Reuse on another cohort:** the separate [APP/PSEN example](evaluations/GENERALIZATION_EXAMPLE_20261117.md) constructs a real three-protein workspace outside the original 99 and replays its actual APP location-agent job. Its self-contained local packet and unchanged source comparisons preserve full source versions, controls and unavailable reference coverage.
- **Reusable deliverables:** [operating instructions](../HOW_TO_USE.md), [local setup](../START_HERE.md), [slides](../output/presentations/BioGraph_Steward_Overview.pptx), [3:58 narrated demonstration](../output/walkthrough/BioGraph_Steward_Walkthrough.mp4), [project report PDF](../output/pdf/BioGraph_Steward_Final_Report.pdf) ([Word copy](../output/docx/BioGraph_Steward_Final_Report.docx)), portable software and [separate graph/source/decision packages](GOLD_SET_INTERFACE.md) explain the methods, results and reuse paths. Neptune mirrors the qualified retained workspace. The native graph and ordinary answers use their saved source files. A retained-graph parity check does not imply that every profile is copied into Neptune.

## Organizer requirements and judging crosswalk

The original brief permits **autonomous or semi-autonomous software**. Its topic is structured-data integration and link prediction: an agent builds contextualized graphs, then the **same graph-learning method** is applied to baseline and enriched graphs to predict held-out experimentally measured relationships. Our paired learner comparisons are complete; they do not demonstrate consistent incremental benefit. The separate historical protein-function study and current agent query-parity check do not replace that endpoint.

The [scientist-access delivery](evaluations/DEMOCRATIZATION_DELIVERY_20261116.md)
makes the existing graph constructor easier to find, with three steps and a
direct operator form entry. The public saved-example version explains local use.
Together with the source-query agent, this demonstrates a focused
semi-autonomous workflow and reusable construction, without requiring graph
syntax. Independent usability and external-cohort validation remain separate
ways to extend that evidence.

| Judging criterion | Delivered evidence | Remaining limitation |
|---|---|---|
| **Usability — 40%**: accessible to non-KG experts; useful contextualized multihop work | A recorded agent with four sequential steps, five task tabs, cited answers, paths up to three hops, readable source inspections and exports. Local cohort construction and configured agent execution provide reusable entry points. | Independent usability and external-cohort studies would extend the demonstrated software evidence. Browser/operator checks are not an intended-user study. Free-text interpretation remains bounded. |
| **Accuracy and pruning — 30%**: remove erroneous or contextually irrelevant data | A retained graph with reversible exclusions of 298 records with obsolete GO identifiers, every keep/remove/unresolved decision and the source baseline. Identifier/type/provenance checks and source eligibility are explicit. Public-reference and predictor assessments retain their controls. | Automatic removal of biologically false app claims and consistent same-learner enriched-graph prediction gains are unestablished. Candidate/accepted are local workflow states. Unresolved records remain retained. The agent's proposals apply no decisions. |
| **Explainability — 20%**: trace why an edge connects its entities | Versioned assertions, source/record locators, complete citation-assertion inspections, model/tool traces, native raw scores, identity proofs, readable conditions, source-reuse flags and recoverable decision history. | Exact imported study lineage and independent-study counts remain unresolved where source metadata is absent. A second database label is not independent evidence. |
| **Standards — 10%**: explicit, if imperfect vocabulary/schema references; consistent and specific representations | Stable UniProt and source identifiers, Biolink categories and mapped predicates, qualified local relations, [bounded KGX validation](STANDARD_EXPORT.md) and [SSSOM mapping audit](EVIDENCE_OVERLAP.md). | Full KGX/Biolink/JSON-LD/PROV interoperability is not established. Known upstream schema/domain conflicts and unsupported mappings remain explicit; local predicates are not silently strengthened to pass validation. |

The organizer's ontology examples distinguish protein-coding genes, proteins and their translation relation. Our demonstrated entities remain source-specific; gene-to-protein links or labels do not establish protein identity. Only vocabularies and mappings used by the actual demonstration should be claimed.
