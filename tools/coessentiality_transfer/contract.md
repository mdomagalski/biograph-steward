# Prospective gene transfer within the published reference

This is one prospective transfer check of the already selected historical
source-qualification and resource-allocation interpretation method. Earlier
98-gene source work and Broad/RA experimental outcomes were consumed. The new
gene–gene reference block is held until the fixed source, controls, code and
this contract are sealed and independently reviewed. This is **not a new
experimental study**, whole-proteome accuracy, independent biological truth or
a statistical significance claim.

## Fixed metadata population and one sample

The original published gene order and native DepMap Entrez header, pinned
reviewed-human registry, and old 98-gene/HGNC/product identities define the
population. Eligibility requires a unique native Entrez matrix index, one
Approved HGNC gene, exactly one consistently qualified canonical reviewed
human UniProt product, no conflicting secondary reviewed candidate, and one
known canonical chromosome. No symbols/aliases/forms are collapsed.

Before drawing, the conservative rule excluded twenty additional genes with
one convenient qualified product plus a conflicting secondary candidate.
Their complete source records are preserved. The resulting reviewed uniquely
mapped subset has 17,231 genes; it is not the whole human proteome. Frame order
is increasing integer Entrez ID. Protocol and frame were sealed before the
single Python SRSWOR draw of 128 genes, seed 2026111302. Inclusion probability is
128/17231. No replacements, alternate samples or source-coverage selection are
allowed. Source/pathway/fitness/TDL data were not eligibility criteria.

The 128 sampled genes define 8,128 unordered pairs, of which 7,648 are
nonsyntenic and 480 same-chromosome. The nonsyntenic domain is primary and
fixed. Top N means top partners **within these 128 sampled genes**, after the
fixed chromosome/measurement mask; it is not genomewide top N or a set of
significance-defined positives.

## Source completion and unchanged native qualification

Only true pathways-only Pharos capture is used, with version, exact requested
identity, pagination, count and transport receipts. Every one of the 128 required responses
must be found and complete. Any required identity/pathway failure, notfound,
truncation or version drift makes the whole comparison UNKNOWN/no-go. A
complete found-target response with zero Wiki rows is valid zero source
context. Other evidence families are explicitly unretrieved, not absent.

Use the actual ordinary protein_cohort Gold graph and its exact native-history
packet; do not fabricate old learner-packet proofs. Normalized context is a
unique gene/exact-source-version incidence projected from the original source
claims through the frozen one-product gene relationships. Source rows and
versions remain preserved outside this simple feature projection.

Historical-supported context reuses the existing native-model parser and
qualification: exact encoded protein xref or compatible explicit gene-level
xref. Gene compatibility never establishes protein/form identity or biological
truth. Unresolved historical records are omitted from this research feature
view while preserved as UNKNOWN source evidence. They are not false biology
or automatic production prune decisions. No current-GMT arm is introduced.

## Exact full-frame controls and native score

Generate twenty count-matched subsets from normalized gene/version links,
seeds 2026111303+i (i=0…19), matching the historical view's unique link count.
Generate twenty author-XSwap historical degree controls, seeds 2026111304+i,
multiplier 10, disjoint integer gene/model namespaces, selfloops False and
antiparallel True. Reuse the pinned author backend. Verify exact simple edge
counts, endpoint types and both degree vectors after the fixed 128-gene
projection. Record all swap diagnostics and adjacency overlap. Do not change
seeds/multiplier or substitute controls after seeing any results.

There are 42 arms: normalized, historical, twenty count controls and twenty
degree controls. No current-GMT, Jaccard, learner or additional method is added.
Identical/empty views or an inability to produce the fixed valid control design
is noninformative/UNKNOWN before new reference capture; do not force a new
sample or a different control algorithm.

The primary source score is native NetworkX 3.7 resource_allocation_index on
each simple undirected gene–model incidence graph, CPU backend explicitly
selected and PYTHONHASHSEED=2026111301. All 128 gene nodes, including isolates,
are present. Each model degree is its fixed full 128-gene arm degree, not a
degree rebuilt per query or chromosome. It is cohort-local observed degree,
not complete biological pathway size. Different revisions/correlated models
are not independent evidence. RA is uncalibrated affinity, possibly above one.
Zero is absent observed shared context, not a biological negative.

## Unchanged reference ranks and full curves

Before new numerical transport, bind the published 80-byte NPY headers,
complete native gene order, expected ETags/lengths and 128 selected matrix
columns. No new selected column overlaps the old 92. Earlier full columns
persisted old–new raw pair bytes, but not this new–new block. Published methods
and illustrative examples were previously read; no claim of complete knowledge
blinding is made. ETags are server validators, not complete-file hashes.

Reuse the native Fortran column reader and strict HTTP 206/range/length/ETag
guards with one common availability ledger; never treat a transport failure
as biological missingness. No full 17,634² matrix or new GLS fit is needed.

Primary ranks are the published unsigned two-sided GLS P, ascending, using the
author's native-symbol lexical upper triangle. Preserve reverse P/sign values;
do not pick a favorable direction, average directions or introduce a threshold.
Both correlation signs may be functional. Native P=0/1 are valid; NaN/Inf or
values outside[0,1] remain UNKNOWN across every source arm. Invalid sign does
not discard a valid unsigned P.

For every gene and N=1…10, use fractional boundary ties summing to exactly N.
Observed weighted score is the sum of reference-selection weights times RA.
Expected score for query g is N×sum(RA(g,h) over its eligible partners)/partner
count. Sum over all 128 queries and report observed/expected enrichment plus
both absolute sums and means. Zero source scores stay in the denominator.
Zero expected score makes enrichment undefined. If any query has fewer than N
usable partners, the entire fixed-frame N point is UNKNOWN; never drop queries,
shrink N, select another chromosome scope or replace genes.

Binary any-shared-context curves use exactly the same source arms, reference
positions, masks, ties and expected-query backgrounds as a labelled diagnostic.
They cannot replace the primary RA endpoint if it is unfavorable. No extra
sign/chromosome/TDL/fitness subgroup search is performed. Same-chromosome native
pair values may remain in the preservation ledger but are not scored here.

Report all 42 arms and every N, including undefined/negative results. Show each
twenty-draw control curve and descriptive minimum/median/maximum envelopes;
they are not confidence intervals or proof of a uniform/mixed null. The
project-specific full-curve rule is fixed: historical enrichment must exceed
normalized and all forty own controls by >1e-12 at every N, with every point
defined. No best-N/arm/draw selection or p-values from correlated N points.

This evaluates within-reference gene transfer and contextual concentration.
Query pairs/directions/control draws are not independent biological replicates.
Relative enrichment may rise while absolute observed affinity falls; preserve
that tradeoff explicitly. No calibrated accuracy, new PPI/causal mechanism,
prediction gain, whole-proteome inference or automatic biological pruning is
claimed. Source/evidence reuse and the historical pan-cancer reference remain
limitations, even when sampling and arithmetic verify correctly.
